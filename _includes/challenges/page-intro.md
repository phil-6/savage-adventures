Wales, with its rugged landscapes and majestic peaks, offers some of the most breathtaking views in the UK. Now, imagine conquering its
three highest summits, all within a span of 24 hours. Sounds exhilarating, doesn’t it? Dive into this thrilling experience and test your
endurance as you scale Yr Wyddfa, Cadair Idris, and Pen Y Fan.

### Highlights

**Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon)**: The highest peak in Wales, Yr Wyddfa stands tall at 1,085 meters. As you ascend, be prepared to be mesmerized by
the panoramic views of the surrounding valleys, lakes, and the distant horizon.

**Cadair Idris**: Nestled in the southern end of the Eryri National Park, Cadair Idris boasts a height of 893 meters. Its unique landscape,
dotted with glacial lakes and rugged terrains, promises an unforgettable hiking experience.

**Pen Y Fan**: The crown jewel of the Bannau Brycheiniog National Park, Pen Y Fan rises to 886 meters. The peak offers a 360-degree view of
the rolling Welsh countryside, making every step of the climb worth it.
